# Code of conduct

Kumquat follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and patient, assume good intent, keep criticism about the work and not the person, and
remember that everyone here is giving their spare time. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not
accepted in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else the project lives.

If someone breaks these rules, use **Report content** on the comment (in its ⋯ menu) or start a
[discussion](https://github.com/organetto8/Kumquat/discussions) asking the maintainer to get in touch
privately. The maintainer may edit or delete comments, lock threads, and block people who keep breaking them.
